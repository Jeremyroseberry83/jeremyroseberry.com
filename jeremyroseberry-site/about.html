About Jeremy

The Short Version

Jeremy Roseberry is a capital advisor, entrepreneur, and thought leader based in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida. He works across private markets, ventures, family office strategy, and real estate. He's building a leadership platform focused on honest stories about success, maturity, and culture.

The Full Story

Jeremy spent 15 years in capital markets and private investments before realizing something: most financial conferences are sterile. Most leadership development is generic. Most organizational culture work misses the mark.

But he also noticed something else: the best leaders are built through stories. Real stories about paralysis and breakthrough, about character under pressure, about knowing when you need help and being brave enough to ask for it.

Today, Jeremy works across multiple streams—all designed around the same principle: help leaders and operators see clearly.

What He Does

Capital Advisory

As a capital partner with Access Global (a 30-country private markets platform), Jeremy sources allocations from HNWIs, family offices, and sovereign funds to sponsors and operators. He's closed deals ranging from $75K to $350K in placement fees.

Entrepreneurship

Jeremy co-founded and runs Roseberry Properties, a referral-only residential and commercial brokerage in South Florida. His wife Kourtney runs Premier Home Watch, a concierge service for luxury homeowners with plans to scale from 8 to 20+ clients.

Strategic Ventures

Jeremy is building GP positions in multiple platforms: a $50M gold mine raise (Camp Birdseed), a life sciences incubator (Helix Capital), and mixed-use real estate (Ceres). He's also an active investor in early-stage ventures with Microsoft/Google acquisition pathways.

Thought Leadership

Beginning 2027, Jeremy is publishing a series of books on leadership, maturity, and real success. He's also developing a speaking practice focused on 7–9 minute keynotes for conferences that need energy and meaning, not just data.

Why Family Matters

Jeremy's wife is Kourtney. Their children are Grant and Amelia. Everything he builds—every investment, every business, every platform—is designed around one principle: family stability comes first. Income stability funds freedom. Freedom funds the ability to say no to things that don't align with what matters most.

What He Believes

Trust the people first. Evaluate the economics. Assess probability. Confirm timing. Check long-term alignment. In that order.

Real success is rare. Most people chase it wrong. Most organizations build it wrong. Most leadership development teaches it wrong.

Everyone needs an adult somewhere. The best leaders know where they're immature and aren't afraid to find a mentor.

Culture is economics. You can't separate them. Every cultural choice has economic implications. Every economic decision shapes culture.

The numbers only matter if they serve something bigger. If you've forgotten why you're building, no amount of revenue will feel like success.
